# Tisilia Brand Asset Policy

This policy applies to the Tisilia names, logos, Tisilia-chan character artwork, and other brand assets distributed under:

`assets/brand/tisilia/`

It is intentionally separate from the MIT License that applies to the Tisilia source code and other materials that are not otherwise marked.

## 1. Purpose

The Tisilia project is open source, but its source-code license and its brand identity serve different purposes.

The MIT License is meant to keep the software broadly reusable.

This policy is meant to allow reasonable use of the Tisilia name, logo, and Tisilia-chan artwork when referring to the project, while avoiding uses that could mislead people about origin, sponsorship, endorsement, or official status.

This policy does not state that every individual brand asset is independently copyrightable in every jurisdiction. Some artwork in this directory was created with generative-AI tools and then selected, directed, edited, resized, composed, or otherwise prepared for the Tisilia project. Any copyright, trademark, unfair-competition, passing-off, contractual, or other rights are asserted only to the extent they exist under applicable law.

## 2. Definitions

- **Tisilia Marks**: `Tisilia`, `Tisilia-chan`, the Tisilia connection-ring logo or motif, and other project-identifying marks in this directory.
- **Tisilia Artwork**: Tisilia-chan illustrations, avatars, banners, social images, and related visual assets in this directory.
- **Brand Assets**: the Tisilia Marks and Tisilia Artwork together.
- **Official Project**: the Tisilia project maintained by kkdev92 and its official repository, packages, releases, documentation, and websites.

## 3. Uses allowed without separate permission

You may use unmodified or proportionally resized Brand Assets for the following purposes, provided the use is truthful and does not imply sponsorship or endorsement:

- Linking to or identifying the Official Project.
- Referring to Tisilia in an article, blog post, presentation, tutorial, review, comparison, or news report.
- Showing Tisilia in documentation that explains how your software integrates with or depends on Tisilia.
- Displaying a Tisilia package, dependency, compatibility, or integration relationship.
- Using screenshots or small excerpts of Brand Assets when discussing the project.
- Community discussion, issue reports, pull requests, release notes, or other good-faith commentary about Tisilia.

When practical, link to the official repository or project page.

When a relationship might otherwise be unclear, a disclaimer such as this is encouraged:

> Tisilia is an independent open-source project. This project is not endorsed by or affiliated with the Tisilia maintainers.

## 4. Uses requiring prior written permission

Please obtain permission from the Tisilia maintainer before using Brand Assets for:

- Merchandise, physical goods, paid stickers, clothing, printed goods, or other commercial products.
- Selling or licensing Tisilia-chan artwork itself.
- Advertising where the Brand Assets are a primary visual identity rather than a factual reference to Tisilia.
- Events, communities, services, or products presented in a way that could reasonably appear official or formally endorsed.
- Material modifications to Tisilia-chan intended for redistribution as an alternate, derived, or competing official-style character.
- Registering or using a domain name, account name, company name, product name, package name, or service name that is confusingly similar to Tisilia or Tisilia-chan.

## 5. Uses not permitted

You may not use Brand Assets:

- To falsely claim that your project, product, service, event, or account is official, sponsored, certified, approved, or endorsed by Tisilia or kkdev92.
- In a way likely to confuse users about the source of software or services.
- As the primary identity of an unrelated or competing product.
- To impersonate the Tisilia project or its maintainer.
- In a deceptive, fraudulent, or misleading manner.
- In connection with unlawful content.
- In a way that intentionally damages the project's reputation through false attribution or misrepresentation.
- To remove or alter required third-party notices that accompany other Tisilia materials.

## 6. Modifications

For permitted editorial, documentation, and integration uses, these changes are allowed without separate permission:

- Proportional resizing.
- Cropping that does not materially change the character or logo.
- Placement on a different background.
- Minor compression or format conversion needed for a target platform.
- Adding surrounding layout, captions, or explanatory text clearly separate from the Brand Asset.

Do not:

- Distort aspect ratio.
- Mirror Tisilia-chan artwork when doing so moves the asymmetric connection-ring hair accessory to the opposite side.
- Change the logo or character in a way that could be mistaken for a new official version.
- Remove provenance or attribution information from files where such information is included as documentation.
